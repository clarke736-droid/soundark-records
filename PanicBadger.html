About the band

Panic Badger are an Amsterdam based 4 piece post-punk/pop band with British, American, and Dutch roots. Formed in 2024, they revel in high principles, fear of labour, and a deep-seated desire to make you feed the billionaires with more of your trickle-up cash. Musically, they sit at the intersection of The Beach Boys, The Velvet Underground, and Pixies, with a sharp pop sensibility and a modern edge.
