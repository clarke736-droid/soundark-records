Danny & the Deadbeats

About the band

Danny & the Deadbeats were an indie punk band from Nottingham, England circa 2004-2008. They only ever did self released recordings at the time. They don't really even have an official band photo, and there are only a few live photos and vids in existence.

The recordings you find here were previously only available on home-produced CDs, and these are more recent remixes. They were a great live act full of energy, now you can discover their somewhat chaotic, fun, and very stupid take on the world!
